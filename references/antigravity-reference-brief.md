## Product Feeling

Lovcore is not a social bookmark app. It is a private AI intelligence archive.

It should feel like:

- mymind for AI/business research
- a private Sequoia/a16z reading room
- a founder's memory wall
- a calm research vault for keynotes, essays, PDFs, clips, screenshots, and notes

## What To Improve Next

1. Main canvas
   - Make the first screen feel quieter and more intentional.
   - The search/save input should feel like the core product, not a normal search bar.
   - The page should immediately communicate: save anything, organize nothing.

2. Cards
   - Replace generic design mock content with AI/business/technology research content.
   - Make article cards feel like investor essay clippings.
   - Make video cards feel like saved keynote/interview moments.
   - Make PDF cards feel like research reports.
   - Make link cards feel like high-signal founder research bookmarks.
   - Make note cards feel like private founder thoughts, not sticky notes.

3. Detail drawer
   - Make the detail view feel like entering a saved intelligence object.
   - AI summary should read like a concise analyst memo.
   - Metadata should include source, type, tags, saved date, key claims, and why it matters.

4. Drag and analyzing states
   - Dragging content in should feel like putting material into a private archive.
   - AI analyzing should feel calm and smart, not like a spinner from a SaaS app.

5. Fix quality issues
   - Remove all encoding glitches.
   - Remove unused Vite/React default assets.
   - Keep both light and dark modes polished.
   - Test desktop and mobile layouts.

## Recommended Copy Tone

Use short, quiet English UI copy:

- Search your mind...
- Save anything
- Organize nothing
- Reading source...
- Extracting signal...
- Building memory card...
- Saved to your private archive
- Same thread
- Key claims
- Why it matters

Avoid fluffy AI language:

- magical
- revolutionary
- unleash
- supercharge
- copilot everything

## Visual North Star

The final prototype should make the user think:

"This is where I would save AI keynotes, founder essays, product screenshots, and half-formed ideas before they disappear."
